PROJECT INFORMATION

How the simulation works

01

Detailed procedural island

Varied terrain, sand and a wet shoreline surround palms, foliage and rocks. Water occupies a separate ring around the island.

02

Camera cutaway and tripod

The transparent body exposes the convex lens, ray paths and sensor. Only the camera moves; the island stays in place.

03

Visible optical geometry

X follows the optical direction. Y and Z show vertical and lateral movement. Rays from one object point converge at one image point.

04

Live inverted sensor

The sensor shows the inverted image formed by the lens. Moving the camera changes its viewpoint and the ray geometry.

05

Capture and processing

The photograph is rendered anew at high resolution and saved upright, in RGB or grayscale, with technical data visible on the PNG.
